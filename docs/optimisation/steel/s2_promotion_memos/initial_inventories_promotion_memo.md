# Initial Inventories Promotion Memo

## Category Purpose In S2

`initial_inventories` define the opening state for bounded buffers such as `DRI` or slab or `WIP`.

## Evidence Status

Current rows are placeholders or toy references only.

Reviewed candidate numerical evidence is missing.

## Unit Conventions

- future executable unit: `tonnes`
- current placeholders are non-executable references only

## Sign Conventions

Inventory levels are positive state quantities.

## Model Role

- missing-evidence category now;
- executable model input later only after review.

## Approval Blockers

- no reviewed public or candidate opening-stock evidence;
- inventory policy choice is unresolved;
- toy placeholders do not count as candidate approval.

## Thesis-Usability Requirements

- explicit opening-inventory policy;
- reviewed evidence or clearly justified policy assumption;
- consistency with terminal-inventory treatment.

## Misuse Red Flags

- carrying toy opening stocks into a public steel run;
- hiding missing inventory evidence behind “neutral” defaults without disclosure;
- treating structural buffer existence as inventory-value approval.
